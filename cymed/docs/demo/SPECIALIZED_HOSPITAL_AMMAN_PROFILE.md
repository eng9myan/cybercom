## 1. At-a-glance

- **Legal / brand name:** The Specialty Hospital — Arabic: المستشفى التخصصي
  (sometimes rendered "Specialty Hospital" or "The Specialty Hospital"). `[web]`
- **Address:** Jaber Ibn Hayyan Street, Al-Shmesani (Shmeisani), Amman,
  Jordan. `[web]`
- **Main line:** +962 6 200 1111 (24/7). Email: `info@specialty-hospital.com`,
  `hello@specialty-hospital.com`. Website: `specialty-hospital.com`. `[web]`
- **Founded:** Company established 1990; hospital officially inaugurated under
  Royal patronage in 1993. `[web]`
- **Ownership:** Private, Jordanian shareholder company. Specific parent
  holding not disclosed publicly on the hospital site. `[web-approx]`
- **Positioning:** Private teaching hospital; regional destination for medical
  tourism (serves patients from ~150 nationalities). `[web]`
- **Beds:** 265 (JCI-accredited private tertiary). `[web]`
- **Operating theatres:** 21 ORs — described as the largest OR complex in
  Jordan's private sector. `[web]`
- **ICU / CCU:** ~30 combined adult critical-care beds (ICU + CCU). `[web]`
- **NICU:** 28 incubators — largest private NICU in Jordan. `[web]`
- **Emergency:** 24/7 Emergency Department + Air Ambulance service. `[web]`
- **Staff:** ~1,000–1,100 employees; ~750 consultants on the medical staff
  panel; ~90 resident doctors; ~343 nurses. `[web-approx]`
- **International accreditation:** JCI — 6 cycles (2008, 2011, 2014, 2017,
  2020, 2023) plus JCI Clinical Care Program Certification as a **Center of
  Excellence for Cardiac Care (AMI + Heart Failure)** — 1st in Jordan,
  7th globally. `[web]`
- **National accreditation:** HCAC (Health Care Accreditation Council of
  Jordan) — 6 cycles (2008, 2010, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2022); HCAC Breast Imaging
  Unit accreditation (renewed through 2025); first private Baby-Friendly
  Hospital in Jordan. `[web]`
- **Other:** MTQUA (medical-tourism), AHA/ASA certified Stroke Center (1st in
  Jordan/ME), ISO 9001 / 15189 / 14001 / 22000 / 45001, King Abdullah II Award
  for Excellence (4th & 5th cycles). `[web]`
- **First-in-Jordan milestones:** CT 512-slice scanner; first silent MRI;
  first laparoscopic donor nephrectomy; first pediatric kidney transplant;
  endoscopic spine surgery; HIPEC; separation of conjoined twins (2021).
  `[web]`

## 4. Insurance panel

The Specialty Hospital publicly states it works with **30+ international
insurance companies** and serves patients from ~150 nationalities. `[web]`

partner listings):

**Government / referral flow:** Royal Medical Services (RMS) and the Ministry
of Health can refer Jordanian nationals to private tertiary centres including
The Specialty Hospital under the standard referral-and-approval workflow.

handled ad-hoc through the admissions / PR office. `[assumption]`

## 5. Digital footprint today — what exists vs gap

### 5.1 What the hospital publishes today (confirmed via `specialty-hospital.com`)
- **Public website** — bilingual (EN / AR), service directory, doctor list,
  news, media library. `[web]`
- **"Request your treatment plan"** — a web form that routes to the medical
  tourism / PR office; not a real-time booking engine. `[web]`
- **Results portal** — the site references logging in with a username and
  password to view laboratory and radiology results. Feature exists but is
  narrow (results retrieval only). `[web]`
- **24/7 call centre** — +962 6 200 1111. `[web]`
