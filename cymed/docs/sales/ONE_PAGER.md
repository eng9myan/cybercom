# CyMed by Cybercom

**The operating system for Middle East healthcare.**

Unified clinical, ancillary, revenue, and patient-facing software. Built in the region, for the region. Deployed as SaaS, on-prem, or hybrid with JO-regional data residency.

---

## The Problem

Hospitals and clinics in JO / KSA / UAE run **5 to 12 disconnected systems**: HIS, LIS, RIS, PACS, pharmacy, ERP, RCM, patient portal, and manual claim submission. Result:

- Claim rejection rates of **15 to 30%** (illustrative, regional average) driven by fragmented data flows into **NPHIES / CCHI**.
- No native Arabic RTL clinical workflows; providers work in English, patients receive English documents.
- E-invoicing (**JoFotara**) and national health records (**Hakeem**) require custom integrations that never ship.
- Ambient documentation, AI clinical decision support, and cross-provider referrals are sold as separate SaaS by separate vendors.

The regional buyer is forced to choose between a **global mega-suite** (Epic, Oracle Health, InterSystems) that ignores local rails, or a **local point solution** (InfoMed, DXware, Bayanat, Alpha) that stops at one department.

---

## The Solution

One platform. **39 modules across 10 clinical and operational groups.** Middle-East-native from day one.

| Layer | What is included |
|---|---|
| Clinical | Reception, ED, ICU, OR, NICU, inpatient, outpatient, nursing |
| Ancillary | Lab (LIS), Imaging (RIS/PACS-lite), Pharmacy (in-patient + retail) |
| Revenue | RCM, NPHIES claims, JoFotara e-invoice, CCHI, HyperPay |
| People | HR, payroll, credentialing, on-call rota |
| Quality | JCI / CBAHI / HCAC dashboards, incident reporting, audit trail |
| Patient | Mobile app, delegated pay, e-prescription, medical-tourism concierge |
| Executive | Real-time KPIs, occupancy, DNFB, denial trends |

**Native Arabic RTL. Multi-currency (JOD / SAR / AED / USD). WHO ICD-11 terminology server built in.**

---

## Six Reasons We Win

1. **Full-stack single platform.** 39 modules on one data model. Competitors sell 3 to 5 modules and integrate the rest — every integration is a project, a cost, and a failure point.
2. **Middle-East-native.** Arabic RTL, JOD / SAR / AED, and **NPHIES, JoFotara, CCHI, SFDA, Hakeem** connectors shipped in the box — not on the roadmap.
3. **Ambient scribe and AI CDS are core**, not a bolt-on. No separate contract, no separate login, no separate BAA.
4. **Cross-provider ecosystem.** Referrals, results, and payer records flow across facilities on the CyMed network. Referring physician sees the discharge summary the same day.
5. **Patient app with delegated pay.** A family member abroad settles a JO patient's bill from the UAE in JOD. No competitor ships this.
6. **Medical-tourism concierge module.** Visa letter, airport pickup, translator, follow-up. Purpose-built for JO and KSA inbound care.

**Plus:** cloud, on-prem, or hybrid — with **JO-regional data residency** for MoH-sensitive workloads.
