**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of `<Effective Date>` (the "Effective Date") by and between:

- **`<Provider Legal Entity>`**, a company organised under the laws of `<Country>`, with its registered office at `<Address>` ("Provider"); and
- **`<Customer Legal Entity>`**, a company organised under the laws of `<Country>`, with its registered office at `<Address>` ("Customer").

Provider and Customer are each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties."

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Services"** means the CyMed software-as-a-service platform and any professional services described in an Order Form.
1.2 **"Order Form"** means a written ordering document referencing this Agreement, signed by both Parties.
1.3 **"Authorised User"** means an individual employed or contracted by Customer and authorised to use the Services on Customer's behalf.
1.4 **"Customer Data"** means all data, including Protected Health Information, uploaded by or on behalf of Customer to the Services.
1.5 **"Documentation"** means the then-current technical documentation for the Services made available by Provider.
1.6 **"Confidential Information"** has the meaning set out in Section 8.
1.7 **"Subscription Term"** means the term set out in the applicable Order Form.
1.8 **"Fees"** means the amounts payable under an Order Form.

## 2. Licence Grant

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement and payment of Fees, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide right during the Subscription Term to access and use the Services solely for its internal business operations and, where clinically applicable, for the direct care of its patients.
2.2 Customer shall not (a) reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive source code from the Services except to the extent expressly permitted by law; (b) sublicense, resell, rent or lease the Services except to Affiliates listed in an Order Form; (c) use the Services to build a competing product; (d) upload malicious code; or (e) exceed the licensed capacity in any billing period without truing up.

## 3. Subscription Term

3.1 Each Order Form has the Subscription Term specified therein.
3.2 Unless otherwise stated, each Subscription Term automatically renews for successive twelve-month terms unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
3.3 Pricing at renewal may be increased by Provider by no more than the greater of (a) five percent (5%) or (b) the year-over-year change in the local consumer price index.

## 4. Fees and Billing

4.1 Fees are set out in the applicable Order Form.
4.2 Invoices are issued monthly or annually in advance as specified in the Order Form. Usage-based overages and true-ups are billed monthly in arrears.
4.3 Payment is due thirty (30) days from invoice date. Late amounts accrue interest at the lower of 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law.
4.4 Fees are exclusive of all taxes. Customer is responsible for VAT, withholding tax, and any similar levies (excluding taxes on Provider's net income).
4.5 Provider may suspend Services for material non-payment on thirty (30) days' written notice and failure to cure. Suspension does not relieve Customer of the obligation to pay outstanding Fees.

## 5. Service Level Agreement

5.1 Provider will provide the Services in accordance with the Service Level Agreement ("SLA") attached as **Exhibit A** and incorporated by reference.

## 6. Uptime Credits

6.1 If Provider fails to meet the monthly uptime target set out in the SLA, Customer's sole and exclusive remedy is a service credit computed as set out in the SLA, applied to the next invoice.
6.2 Aggregate credits in any month may not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of that month's recurring subscription fees for the affected edition.

## 7. Intellectual Property

7.1 As between the Parties, Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Services, the Documentation, and all improvements, modifications and derivative works thereof, including all intellectual property rights.
7.2 Customer retains all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data.
7.3 Feedback provided by Customer regarding the Services may be used by Provider without restriction; no such use shall confer any ownership right on Provider in Customer Data.
7.4 Provider may generate de-identified statistical and analytical data derived from Customer Data, provided that such data is de-identified in accordance with applicable law and cannot reasonably be used to identify any patient or Customer.

## 8. Confidentiality

8.1 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
8.2 Recipient shall (a) protect Confidential Information with the same care it uses for its own confidential information (and no less than reasonable care); (b) use it only to perform this Agreement; and (c) disclose it only to personnel with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as those in this Agreement.
8.3 Obligations survive for five (5) years after termination, except for trade secrets and Protected Health Information, which are protected for as long as they remain confidential or as required by law.

## 9. Data Ownership and Location

9.1 Customer is and remains the owner and, where applicable, the controller of Customer Data.
9.2 Provider processes Customer Data as a processor / business associate under the Data Processing Agreement (Exhibit B) and Business Associate Agreement (Exhibit C, if applicable).
9.3 Primary hosting location is `<Region — e.g., KSA (Riyadh), UAE (Dubai), EU (Frankfurt), US (Virginia)>`. Provider will not transfer Customer Data outside the primary region without prior written notice, except for standard operational sub-processing listed in the DPA sub-processor table and, in emergencies, for the sole purpose of maintaining continuity of care with subsequent notification.
9.4 On termination Customer may export Customer Data in a documented format for ninety (90) days; thereafter Provider will delete Customer Data in accordance with the DPA, subject to legal retention obligations.

## 10. Warranties

10.1 Each Party warrants that it has the full authority to enter into this Agreement.
10.2 Provider warrants that (a) the Services will materially conform to the Documentation; and (b) Provider will not knowingly introduce malicious code.
10.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND PROVIDER DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED OR STATUTORY, INCLUDING WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT, TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW.

## 11. Indemnification

11.1 **Provider indemnity.** Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services, as delivered by Provider and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe such third party's patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret rights, and shall pay damages finally awarded (or amounts in an approved settlement).
11.2 **Customer indemnity.** Customer shall defend Provider against any third-party claim arising from (a) Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement or law, or (b) any clinical decision made by Customer's personnel.
11.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party must (a) give prompt written notice; (b) tender sole control of the defence and settlement; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.
11.4 **Mutual cap.** Each Party's aggregate liability under this Section 11, and under this Agreement as a whole (whether in contract, tort, or otherwise), is capped at the total Fees paid or payable by Customer to Provider in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This cap does not apply to (i) either Party's indemnification obligations for third-party IP infringement claims, (ii) breach of confidentiality, (iii) willful misconduct or fraud, (iv) Customer's payment obligations, or (v) liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.
11.5 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, EXCEPT THAT THIS EXCLUSION SHALL NOT APPLY TO THE CARVE-OUTS IN SECTION 11.4.

## 12. Term and Termination

12.1 This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for so long as any Order Form remains in effect.
12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form for material breach by the other Party not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice.
12.3 Either Party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent or subject to insolvency proceedings not stayed within sixty (60) days.
12.4 Termination does not relieve Customer of the obligation to pay Fees accrued through the termination date.

## 13. Transition Assistance

13.1 On termination or expiration, and provided Customer has paid all undisputed Fees, Provider will (a) maintain read-only access to the Services for ninety (90) days for data export; (b) upon written request, provide reasonable transition assistance at Provider's then-current professional-services rates; and (c) at Customer's option, provide one full database export in a documented open format at no additional charge.

## 14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

14.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of `<Governing Law — placeholder options: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia; Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan; England and Wales; State of Delaware, USA; other as agreed>`, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
14.2 **Dispute resolution.** The Parties will first attempt to resolve disputes in good faith through escalation. Unresolved disputes will be finally resolved by arbitration under `<ICC / DIAC / SCCA / LCIA / other>` rules, seat `<Seat>`, in English, before one or three arbitrators.
14.3 Notwithstanding the foregoing, either Party may seek injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect its intellectual property or Confidential Information.

## 15. Miscellaneous

15.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, provided the assignee assumes all obligations.
15.2 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses above or as later designated.
15.3 **Force majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control.
15.4 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Order Forms and Exhibits, is the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
15.5 **Order of precedence.** In the event of conflict: (1) DPA / BAA, (2) SLA, (3) Order Form, (4) main body of this Agreement, (5) Documentation.
