# Business Associate Agreement (BAA) — CyMed Template

**Use:** every US-connected customer must sign a BAA with CyMed before any PHI flows.

---

## Parties

- **Covered Entity ("CE")**: [Customer name, address]
- **Business Associate ("BA")**: CyMed Healthcare Systems, [address]

## Purpose

BA provides electronic health record and revenue-cycle software services to CE. BA will create, receive, maintain, or transmit Protected Health Information ("PHI") on behalf of CE.

## Definitions

Terms have meanings from 45 CFR §§ 160.103, 164.304, 164.501.

## Obligations of BA

1. **Uses & Disclosures** — BA will use/disclose PHI only as permitted or required by this BAA or law.
2. **Safeguards** — BA implements administrative, physical, and technical safeguards per 45 CFR § 164.308-312.
3. **Subcontractors** — BA obtains written assurances from every subcontractor.
4. **Reports** — BA reports any Security Incident or Breach within 5 business days of discovery.
5. **Access** — BA provides PHI to CE within 15 business days on request.
6. **Amendment** — BA amends PHI within 30 days of CE request.
7. **Accounting** — BA maintains an accounting of disclosures for 6 years.
8. **Audit** — BA makes internal practices available to HHS.
9. **Return / destruction** — On termination, BA returns or destroys all PHI. If infeasible, protections continue.

## Permitted uses

- Perform functions described in the master service agreement.
- Data aggregation services per 45 CFR § 164.504(e)(2)(i)(B).
- De-identification per § 164.514.

Effective [date]. Terminates on termination of MSA or on 30-day notice for material breach.
