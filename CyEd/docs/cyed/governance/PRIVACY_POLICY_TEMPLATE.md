## 1. Who we are

[School Name] ("the School", "we", "us") uses the CyEd platform to manage student enrolment, academic records, attendance, health and wellbeing information, billing, and communication with families. This policy explains what personal information we collect through CyEd, why, and what your rights are.

Contact for privacy questions: [privacy officer name/email].

## 2. What we collect

- **Student information**: name, date of birth, gender, residential address, enrolment history, academic records (grades, assessments, report cards), attendance, and — where relevant — health, medical and wellbeing information, disability/support-plan information, and behaviour records.
- **Statutory identifiers**: Unique Student Identifier (USI), state student number, and — for national collections — de-identified demographic categories (Indigenous status, language background, socio-economic indicators) reported to government education authorities as required by law.
- **Family/guardian information**: name, contact details, relationship to student, billing/household information for fee purposes.
- **Staff information**: name, contact details, employment/contract details, qualifications, for HR and payroll purposes — this section applies to staff as data subjects, not to student data.

## 3. How we use it

- Deliver core educational services: enrolment, timetabling, gradebook, attendance, communication.
- Statutory reporting to government education authorities (NCCD, NAPLAN participation, school census) — see §7.
- Fee billing and collection.
- Health and duty-of-care response (sick bay, medication administration, emergency contact).
- Where enabled, AI-assisted tutoring for students: **student prompts are stripped of identifying information (name, email, phone) before being sent to the underlying AI model**, the AI never gives students a direct answer to an assessed task (guiding questions only), and every AI-generated artefact is reviewed by a teacher before being relied on.

## 4. Who we share it with

- Government education authorities, where required by law (statutory reporting, §7).
- Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract (e.g. the platform operator, payment processors where fee payment is enabled) — these providers are contractually bound to use the data only for the purpose we specify.
- We do **not** sell personal information, and do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

## 6. Security

Personal information is protected by encryption (sensitive fields are encrypted at rest), role-based access control (staff only see what their role requires), multi-factor authentication for staff accessing sensitive systems, and an audit trail of access to sensitive records. See the School's System Security Plan for technical detail (available on request to [contact]).

## 7. Statutory reporting

As an Australian school, we are required to report certain information to government authorities, including:
- **NCCD** (Nationally Consistent Collection of Data on School Students with Disability)
- **NAPLAN** participation records
- **School census** data (ABS/ACARA)

These exports contain the minimum information required by the relevant collection and are not used for any purpose beyond the statutory requirement.

## 8. Your rights

You may request access to, or correction of, your (or your child's) personal information by contacting [privacy officer]. Where a student leaves the school, their record is retained for a period consistent with our Data Retention Schedule and then de-identified — see that document for the current default window.

## 9. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact [privacy officer] in the first instance. If unresolved, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): oaic.gov.au.
